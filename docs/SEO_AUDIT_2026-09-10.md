# Red & Yellow website SEO audit

**Audit date:** 10 September 2026  
**Site:** https://www.redandyellow.co.za  
**Pages in sitemap:** 561  
**Pages returning HTTP 200:** 435  
**Overall status:** Foundational SEO is present, but the site has page-template consistency gaps that should be fixed before treating every programme and content page as search-ready.

This is the page-level audit that was previously missing from the ebook. It crawled every URL published in the XML sitemap and checked crawlability, titles, descriptions, canonical links, headings, social tags, structured data, image alt text and mobile viewport markup.

### Warnings

- 107 titles are shorter than 50 characters and 246 exceed 60. Keep the primary phrase and qualification type while removing repeated boilerplate.
- 7 title strings are duplicated across multiple pages. Duplicate groups: Red & Yellow (9); About Red & Yellow Creative School of Business | Our Approach (2); Bursaries | Red & Yellow (2); Red & Yellow Creative School of Business expands into Mauritius and be (2); The connection between creative thinking practical output and theory | (2); The Red & Yellow Creative School of Business named the 1 school in the (2); The rise and rise of content creators in the world of AI | Red & Yello (2)
- 57 descriptions are shorter than 120 characters and 71 exceed 170. Rewrite at the template level so every page earns a useful search snippet.
- 2 pages have no H1 and 425 have more than one H1. Enforce one descriptive H1 per page; use H2 for sections.
- 435 pages lack one or more core Open Graph tags and 435 lack the core Twitter card tags. Add template defaults and page-specific overrides.
- 435 pages expose no JSON-LD structured data. Add Organization/WebSite site-wide and BreadcrumbList; add Course or EducationalOccupationalProgram where the page content supports it.
- 310 images have missing or empty alt text across the crawled pages. Prioritise hero, programme and accreditation images; mark decorative images explicitly empty.

### Passing signals

- robots.txt returned HTTP 200 and declares the sitemap: https://www.redandyellow.co.za/sitemap.xml.
- The XML sitemap contains 561 URLs, so the site has a discoverable URL inventory.
- 435 of 435 live pages declare a mobile viewport.
- The homepage has a descriptive title and H1, and its visible content clearly describes accredited programmes, online study and campus study. ([homepage](https://www.redandyellow.co.za/))

## Recommended fixes

1. Fix sitemap hygiene first: remove stale URLs, resolve non-200 responses and make redirect targets canonical.
2. Create one metadata contract for programme, category, blog and corporate templates: unique title, 150–160 character description, self-canonical, Open Graph/Twitter tags, one H1 and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD.
3. Add programme structured data only where the visible page contains the corresponding course facts; do not mark up prices or accreditation that the page does not show.
4. Improve internal linking from category pages to every programme and from programme pages to the relevant application path, delivery mode and related courses.
5. Add descriptive alt text and explicit dimensions to hero and card images; lazy-load below-fold media.
6. Re-run this crawl after each template release and reconcile the sitemap URL count, HTTP status, metadata coverage and structured-data coverage.

## Representative catalogue observations
